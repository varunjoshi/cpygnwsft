Frequently Asked Questions

Solar Panel Installation in Kopargaon - FAQ

Common questions answered for Kopargaon residential and commercial solar buyers.

How much central government solar subsidy do Kopargaon residents get?
Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, homeowners in Kopargaon receive ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and a maximum central subsidy of ₹78,000 for 3kW and higher rooftop solar systems.
Who handles MSEDCL net metering connection in Kopargaon?
Sunlight Energies Solution handles end-to-end MSEDCL documentation, online portal submission, bi-directional net meter testing, structural stability certificates, and grid commissioning for Kopargaon solar installations.
What solar panel brands and warranties are available?
We supply and install ALMM-approved Tier-1 solar panels from TATA Power Solar, Waaree Energies, and Adani Solar with 25-to-30-year performance warranties, paired with high-efficiency Polycab and Growatt solar inverters.
